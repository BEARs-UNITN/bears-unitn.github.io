## The problem

Development and validation of a strategy to combine complementary modalities and/or feature representations, improving robustness, interpretability, and transferability across clinical tasks with heterogeneous or partially available data.

## What you will do

As in every MSc thesis at BEARLab, you will:

- develop a **novel module, neural architecture or learning strategy** that addresses an open challenge in medical imaging (not a plain replication of existing methods);
- **validate** it in one or more clinically relevant application domains, on established medical imaging benchmarks.

## Possible application domains

Radiology (miscellaneous) · Neuro-radiology · Neuro-oncology · Ophthalmology

## Example to follow

*Modality-Aware Token Interaction (MATI)*: a module that structures multimodal interaction within a single token stream by partitioning the embedding space into modality-aligned subspaces, integrated into a neural backbone (e.g. UNETR) and validated on an established benchmark (e.g. BraTS for multimodal brain tumour segmentation), with optional generalisation to more backbones and benchmarks.

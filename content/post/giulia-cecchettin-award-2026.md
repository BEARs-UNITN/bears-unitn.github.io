[Letizia Girardi](/authors/letizia-girardi/)'s Master's thesis has received two recognitions:

- the **Giulia Cecchettin Award** for the Information Engineering macro-sector, in the competition promoted by the **Collegio Ingegneri Venezia** (26 February 2026). On 18 April 2026 she presented the awarded thesis at the **7th Excellence Engineers Conference**;
- an **Honorable Mention** at the **20th Bernardo Nobile Award**, Area Science Park, Trieste.

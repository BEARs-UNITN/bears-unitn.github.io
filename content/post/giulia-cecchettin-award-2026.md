[Letizia Girardi](/authors/letizia-girardi/) received the **Giulia Cecchettin Award** for the Information Engineering macro-sector for her Master's thesis, in the competition promoted by the **Collegio Ingegneri Venezia** (26 February 2026).

On 18 April 2026 she presented the awarded thesis at the **7th Excellence Engineers Conference**.

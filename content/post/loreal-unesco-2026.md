On 15 June 2026 in Milan, Selene Tomassini received the **L'Oréal-UNESCO "For Women in Science" Young Talents Italia 2026** award, granted by L'Oréal Italia and the Italian National Commission for UNESCO, for her project **"mAInds: modality-aware AI for neuro-oncological decision support"**.

**MedicinAI** is a cycle of seminars and an international workshop on artificial intelligence in medicine, organized by the BEAR group.

- **17 Oct 2025** · Seminar I: *AI and Clinical Data: From Fragmentation to a Unified Vision* · Prof. Paolo Soda (Università Campus Bio-Medico di Roma)
- **6 Nov 2025** · Seminar II: *AI Supporting Physicians: Diagnosis, Therapy, and Personalized Care* · Prof. Riccardo Bellazzi (Università di Pavia)
- **25 Nov 2025** · Seminar III: *Reliability and Transparency in Healthcare AI* · Prof. Carlo Bottari and Prof. Federico Laus (Università di Bologna)
- **4 Dec 2025** · Workshop: *AI and Decision Support in Healthcare: From Data to Clinical Impact* · Prof. Henning Müller (HES-SO & University of Geneva) and Prof. Katja Bühler (VRVis Vienna Research Center for Visual Computing)

More on our [Events page](/events/) and on the [MedicinAI website](https://sites.google.com/unitn.it/medicinai).
